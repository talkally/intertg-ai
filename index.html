02 — The difference

Most IT providers resell AI. We build it.

Reselling a subscription is not the same as owning the outcome. We design, deploy and operate the platform itself — which is why we can answer questions about where your data went.

On infrastructure you own

The models run inside your own environment, on your premises or in your own cloud tenancy. Nothing is sent to a public AI service.

On your data

Answers come from your own documents and systems, with your existing directory deciding who may see what.

Without per-seat cost

No charge per question, per seat or per user. The cost is the infrastructure, and you already own most of it.

It is supported the same way as the rest of your IT: same team, same change discipline, same phone number.
